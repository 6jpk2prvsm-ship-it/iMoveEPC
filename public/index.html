Before you book

EPC questions.

What does an EPC tell me?

It gives your property an energy efficiency rating from A to G and suggests ways to improve its efficiency.

How can I check whether I already have an EPC?

Search for your property on the GOV.UK energy certificate register.

How much will it cost?

Share your property details and Marc will confirm the price before you arrange a visit.

Which postcodes do you cover?
